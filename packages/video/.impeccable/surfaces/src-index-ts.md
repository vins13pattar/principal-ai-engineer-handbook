# Surface brief: module videos (Remotion, 1920x1080)

## Scope and visitor mode

Read, with a hook. The viewer came to understand one module of the handbook in 5 to 8 minutes. The world owns the frame (neatline, cartouche, route progress, plate number, ground). The diagram at the centre stays calm and readable.

## Audience, job, proof, constraints

Senior AI engineers on a laptop, focused. Job: understand the central decision and where it breaks. Proof: the handbook's own diagrams and claims. Constraint: laptop-readable type, no blank frames, cues tied to narration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each module is a chart plate in an Atlas of Engineering. The system is a territory the viewer is walked across, and the narration lights the route. Refuses the title-over-empty-screen template and the equal-cards slide.
OWN-WORLD: Nautical night-chart palette. Deep navy sea (#0E0C33 to #14113F), indigo land and region fills with authored contour lines, pale chart ink (#C6CBFF family), one amber route colour for the active path, coral only for failure. Neatline double rule with graticule ticks, a cartouche for the headline, a legend that names the symbols in use. Type: a serif display with character for headlines and place names, a workhorse humanist sans for labels, mono only for code and coordinates.
STORY: The viewer feels a failure first, then is walked across the system until the failure makes sense and its cure is clear, and leaves with three statements to remember.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Frame 0 is never empty. The scene's full chart is already drawn in ghost lines; narration lights places and routes one by one. The video opens on a failure replayed on the map before any title appears.
FORM: Atlas plate, user-pinned direction (seed: none, pinned by the user).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; run the detector over the changed sources, review the rendered frames, and write DESIGN.md from the built world.
