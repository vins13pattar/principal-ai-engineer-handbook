The handbook doesn't keep a separate bank of interview trivia. Every **Interview Questions**
section lives inside the [Learn](/Principal-AI-Engineer-Interview-Handbook/learn/) module or [Architecture](/Principal-AI-Engineer-Interview-Handbook/architecture/) page whose
material actually answers it — a question about health-aware routing sits in the module that
explains health-aware routing, next to the failure modes and trade-offs that make the answer
credible.

This section is the index across that material, organized by interview format instead of by
topic, plus the tracks that don't fit neatly under a single module: system design rounds and
leadership/behavioral rounds.

## Tracks

No interview tracks are published yet — see the [Roadmap](/Principal-AI-Engineer-Interview-Handbook/roadmap/) for the authoring schedule.
Once Learn modules exist, this section will index their embedded `<InterviewQuestion>` /
`<InterviewAnswer>` pairs by track (coding, system design, architecture deep-dive, leadership)
rather than duplicate them.
