A cheat sheet is scoped to an **activity under time pressure** — a round you are about to walk
into, an incident you have just been paged for — not to a technology. Per-technology summaries
belong in [Reference](/reference/), and duplicating them here at lower fidelity would help nobody
and double the maintenance surface.

Each sheet answers four questions, enforced in CI: when to reach for it, the order to work in, the
values worth not deriving under pressure, and what going wrong looks like. The print stylesheet
hides navigation chrome, so `Cmd/Ctrl+P` produces a clean page.

## Published

| Sheet | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [Design Round](/cheatsheets/sheets/design-round/) | You have 45 minutes and a system design prompt |
