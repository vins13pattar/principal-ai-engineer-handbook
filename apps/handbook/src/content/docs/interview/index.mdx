The handbook doesn't keep a separate bank of interview trivia. Every **Interview Questions**
section lives inside the [Learn](/learn/) module or [Architecture](/architecture/) page whose
material actually answers it — a question about health-aware routing sits in the module that
explains health-aware routing, next to the failure modes and trade-offs that make the answer
credible.

This section is the index across that material, organized by interview format instead of by
topic, plus the tracks that don't fit neatly under a single module: system design rounds and
leadership/behavioral rounds.

## Tracks

| Track | Round it prepares for |
| --- | --- |
| [AI Infrastructure System Design](/interview/tracks/ai-infrastructure-system-design/) | The 45-minute design round: a method, seven candidate systems mapped to reference architectures, and the failure patterns specific to AI system design |
| [AI Systems Coding](/interview/tracks/ai-systems-coding/) | The applied coding round: the concurrency and correctness components worth writing from an empty file, the traps that fail most answers, and the lab source where each one exists finished |
| [Technical Leadership](/interview/tracks/technical-leadership/) | The leadership round: why it is a technical round conducted on your own history, the decisions worth being able to reconstruct, and the failure patterns that cost strong candidates the loop |

Ninety-odd questions are already embedded across [Learn](/learn/) and [Architecture](/architecture/),
next to the material that answers them. Tracks route to that material by interview format; they do
not copy it. All three rounds a Principal or Staff loop is built from are now covered.

## Decision models

Questions on decision models — whether a model's stated confidence can carry an escalation
threshold, and what it costs when it cannot — sit in
[Module 4](/learn/modules/04-ai-infrastructure/) and
[Module 5](/learn/modules/05-agent-engineering/), and are not yet routed from any track above. They
are the likeliest follow-up when a design-round answer puts a fast model in front of an LLM. The
[Calibrated Decision Tier](/architecture/systems/calibrated-decision-tier/) design review has the
measured numbers to answer with.
